Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the School Integrated Program (ESIP)?

ESIP combines Class XI–XII Board education with JEE/NEET preparation under one roof and one timetable, eliminating the stress of attending separate evening coaching.

Are scholarships available for admission?

Yes, students can appear for our Scholarship-cum-Admission Test or apply based on their Class X Board / Olympiad performance for up to 90% tuition fee waiver.

How are parents updated about student performance?

Test scores, attendance, and rank analytics are shared directly with parents after every fortnightly test, followed by regular Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs).
